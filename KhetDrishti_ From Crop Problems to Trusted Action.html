Understand crop problems, explore reviewed guidance, and connect with agricultural expertise, even when connectivity is limited.

A spot on a leaf. A whole season at stake.

Yellowing, spots and wilting can come from disease, pests, nutrients or water. A small farmer often has to decide quickly, and the nearest expert may be a long way off. The first step is not a verdict. It is a clearer picture and someone to ask.

Three moves from worry to a next step

Read safe guidance, or send the case to an agricultural expert and see their reply here.
